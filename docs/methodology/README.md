# 📐 Methodology

The regulatory basis of CCR-K-SFTs: the chosen framework and its version,
decisions with their primary sources, assumptions, and the numerical fixture
contract. **No method is defined yet.** The open product and methodology
decisions are listed in
[REPOSITORY_STRUCTURE.md](../REPOSITORY_STRUCTURE.md#-open-product-and-methodology-decisions).

This directory will hold, from [#8](https://github.com/danielep71/CCR-K-SFTs/issues/8):

- the jurisdiction and rule version, with the date and a citation of each
  primary source;
- each closed decision, its source and its consequences for inputs and
  results;
- assumptions and unresolved scope questions, stated as such;
- the fixture schema, tolerances and the rules that separate illustrative
  examples from validated benchmarks.

Rules:

- every formula, parameter or threshold used later cites its primary source
  here;
- no derivative SA-CCR content, from K-SACCR or elsewhere, is a default for
  SFTs;
- source documents are cited, not copied, unless their licence allows it.
