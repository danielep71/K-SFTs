# 🤝 Code of conduct

K-SFTs is my independent personal project. Anyone who opens an issue,
comments, reviews or contributes here is expected to keep the exchange
useful and respectful.

## ✅ Expected

- Be courteous and assume good faith; criticise the work, not the person.
- Stay on topic: issues and pull requests are for this repository's work.
- Use synthetic data only, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) require.

## 🚫 Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or repeated off-topic or promotional posts.
- Publishing anyone's private information, or confidential data of any
  employer or client.

## 📨 Reporting

Report a problem privately, by email to the address in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#-reporting-a-vulnerability), with the subject
**Conduct report — K-SFTs**, and include links to what you report. I read
every report and keep it confidential.

## ⚖️ Enforcement

I decide what to do: a reminder, hiding or deleting the content, locking a
thread, or blocking an account from the repository. A vulnerability is never
reported here but through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
